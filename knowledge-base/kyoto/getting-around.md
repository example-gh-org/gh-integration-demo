# Getting Around Kyoto

Kyoto is flatter and more compact than Tokyo, but its sights are spread out.

## Options

- **Buses** - the backbone of the network; a day pass is good value.
- **Subway** - two lines cross the city centre quickly.
- **Cycling** - the flat centre is pleasant to ride, with rentals widely available.

## Reaching Temples
Many famous temples sit on the wooded edges of the city, so plan bus connections in advance.
