# Kinkaku-ji

Kinkaku-ji, the Golden Pavilion, is one of Kyoto's most photographed sights.

## What to See

The top two floors are covered in gold leaf, mirrored in the pond in front. A one-way garden path loops past the best viewpoints.

## Visiting Tips

Arrive at opening or late in the afternoon to avoid the heaviest crowds, and allow about an hour for the circuit.
