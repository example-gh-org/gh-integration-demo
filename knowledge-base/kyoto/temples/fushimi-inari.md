# Fushimi Inari

Fushimi Inari Taisha is famous for the thousands of vermilion torii gates that tunnel up the mountainside.

## What to See

The gate-lined paths climb Mount Inari past smaller shrines. The full loop to the summit and back takes two to three hours.

## Visiting Tips

The shrine is open around the clock and is free; come early or at dusk for near-empty gates and good photos.
