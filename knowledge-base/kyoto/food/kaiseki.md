# Kaiseki in Kyoto

Kaiseki is a refined multi-course meal that reaches its highest form in Kyoto.

## What to Expect

A sequence of small, seasonal courses balancing flavour, texture, and presentation, rooted in the tea ceremony tradition.

## Booking

The best restaurants book out well ahead and may require a reservation through a ryokan or concierge. For a lighter budget, seek out shojin ryori, the Buddhist vegetarian equivalent.
