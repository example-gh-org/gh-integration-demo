# Day Trip to Hakone

Hakone is a hot-spring resort area in the mountains, popular for its onsen and Mount Fuji views.

## The Loop

A classic day combines the mountain railway, the ropeway over the Owakudani volcanic valley, and a boat across Lake Ashi.

## What to See

- **Onsen** - day-use hot springs are widely available.
- **Open-Air Museum** - sculpture set against the hills.
- **Mount Fuji views** - clearest in the cooler months.

## Getting There

The Odakyu Romancecar from Shinjuku is the most direct route.


## Getting around

The Hakone Free Pass covers the loop of railway, ropeway, and sightseeing boat, so buy it before starting the circuit.
