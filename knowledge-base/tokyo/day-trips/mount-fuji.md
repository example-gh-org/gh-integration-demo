# Day Trip to Mount Fuji

Mount Fuji is Japan's highest and most iconic peak, visible from Tokyo on clear days and an easy day trip in its own right.

## Where to Go

- **Kawaguchiko** - the most accessible of the Fuji Five Lakes, with classic reflected views.
- **Chureito Pagoda** - the postcard pagoda-and-Fuji vantage point.
- **5th Station** - the highest point reachable by road.

## Climbing

The official climbing season runs in July and August; outside it the trails are closed and dangerous.

## Getting There

Express buses from Shinjuku reach Kawaguchiko in about two hours.
