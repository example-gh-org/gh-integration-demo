# Day Trip to Nikko

Nikko is a mountain town north of Tokyo, famous for its ornate shrines set among cedar forest.

## What to See

- **Toshogu** - a lavishly decorated shrine complex and UNESCO site.
- **Lake Chuzenji** - a highland lake above the town.
- **Kegon Falls** - a dramatic waterfall near the lake.

## Getting There

Take the Tobu line from Asakusa or the JR line; allow a full day for the round trip.
