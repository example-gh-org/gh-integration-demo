# Day Trip to Kamakura

Kamakura is a seaside town south of Tokyo, once the political capital of medieval Japan.

## What to See

- **Great Buddha** - the giant bronze Daibutsu at Kotoku-in.
- **Hase-dera** - a hillside temple with garden and sea views.
- **Enoden tram** - a charming local line running along the coast.

## Getting There

The JR Yokosuka line reaches Kamakura in under an hour from Tokyo Station.
