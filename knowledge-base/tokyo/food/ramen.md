# Ramen in Tokyo

Ramen is Tokyo's definitive fast meal, with countless shops each guarding their own broth.

## Ordering

Most shops use a ticket vending machine at the entrance - choose your bowl, pay, and hand the ticket to the counter.

## Styles to Try

- **Shoyu** - clear soy-based broth, the Tokyo classic.
- **Tonkotsu** - rich, milky pork-bone broth.
- **Tsukemen** - thick noodles served separately for dipping.

## Etiquette

Slurping is welcome, seats are for eating quickly, and water is usually self-serve.
