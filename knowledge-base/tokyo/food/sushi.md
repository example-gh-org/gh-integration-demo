# Sushi in Tokyo

Sushi in Tokyo spans every price point, from conveyor-belt chains to counters with months-long waiting lists.

## Where to Eat

- **Kaiten-zushi** - conveyor-belt restaurants, cheap and beginner-friendly.
- **Omakase counters** - the chef chooses; sit at the counter and eat each piece promptly.
- **Toyosu Market** - early-morning sushi breakfasts beside the wholesale fish market.

## Etiquette

Eat each piece in one bite, dip fish-side down, and ask for recommendations rather than over-ordering.
