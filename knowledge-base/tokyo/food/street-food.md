# Tokyo Street Food

Street food in Tokyo is clustered around temple approaches, festivals, and a few dedicated lanes rather than spread across every corner.

## What to Try

- **Takoyaki** - molten octopus dumplings, griddled to order.
- **Taiyaki** - fish-shaped cakes filled with sweet red bean or custard.
- **Yakitori** - charcoal-grilled chicken skewers, best from a smoky stall.
- **Melonpan** - a sweet, crisp-topped bun, freshly baked near Sensoji.

## Where to Find It

Nakamise-dori in Asakusa and the lanes around Ueno are reliable. Many stalls are cash-only, so carry coins.
