# Where to Eat in Tokyo

Tokyo has more Michelin stars than any other city, but some of the best meals come from the most unassuming places.

## Convenience Stores

Don't overlook konbini. The onigiri, egg sandwiches, and fried chicken at 7-Eleven, Lawson, and FamilyMart are genuinely excellent and cost very little.

## Standing Bars and Ramen

Tachinomi (standing bars) and ramen counters are a cheap, fast way to eat well. Many ramen shops use a ticket vending machine at the entrance — buy your ticket first, then sit down.

## Booking High-End Restaurants

The most sought-after sushi and kaiseki restaurants book out weeks in advance and often only accept reservations through a hotel concierge. Plan ahead if there is somewhere specific you want to try.
