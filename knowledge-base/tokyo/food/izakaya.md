# Izakaya in Tokyo

An izakaya is a casual pub serving small shared plates alongside drinks - the heart of an evening out.

## How It Works

- Order drinks first; a small **otoshi** (seating snack) is usually charged automatically.
- Share several small plates across the table rather than one main each.
- Look under railway arches and in narrow alleys for the most characterful spots.

## What to Order

Edamame, grilled fish, karaage, yakitori, and whatever the day's specials board lists.
