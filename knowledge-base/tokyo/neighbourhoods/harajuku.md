# Harajuku

Harajuku is the centre of Tokyo youth fashion and street style, bordered by some of the city's grandest green space.

## What to See

- **Takeshita Street** - dense, colourful, and crowded with fashion and crepe stands.
- **Omotesando** - a tree-lined avenue of architect-designed flagship boutiques.
- **Meiji Shrine** - a forested Shinto shrine just beside Harajuku Station.

## Getting There

Harajuku Station is on the JR Yamanote line, next to Yoyogi Park.
