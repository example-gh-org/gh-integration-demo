# Asakusa

Asakusa is Tokyo's most traditional neighbourhood, centred on the Sensoji Temple complex.

## What to See

- **Sensoji** - Tokyo's oldest temple. Enter through the Kaminarimon gate with its giant red lantern.
- **Nakamise-dori** - the approach to the temple, lined with craft and snack stalls.
- **Sumida Park** - riverside walks with views across to the Tokyo Skytree.

## Getting There

Asakusa Station is on the Ginza and Asakusa subway lines. It is an easy add-on to a morning in Ueno.
