# Ginza

Ginza is Tokyo's upmarket shopping and dining district, where flagship stores and historic department stores sit side by side.

## What to See

- **Department stores** - Mitsukoshi and Wako anchor the main crossing.
- **Chuo-dori** - closed to traffic on weekend afternoons for a pedestrian promenade.
- **Galleries and theatres** - including the Kabuki-za for traditional theatre.

## Getting There

Ginza Station is served by the Ginza, Marunouchi, and Hibiya subway lines.
