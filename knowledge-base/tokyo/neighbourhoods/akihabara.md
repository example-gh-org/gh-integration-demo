# Akihabara

Akihabara, or Akiba, is the global capital of electronics, anime, and gaming culture.

## What to See

- **Multi-floor electronics stores** - from cutting-edge gadgets to obscure components.
- **Anime and manga shops** - several buildings stacked floor to ceiling with collectibles.
- **Retro arcades** - classic cabinets and rhythm games over multiple storeys.

## Getting There

Akihabara Station is on the JR Yamanote and Sobu lines. The Electric Town exit drops you in the middle of the action.
