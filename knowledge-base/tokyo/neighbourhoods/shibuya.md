# Shibuya

Shibuya is Tokyo's youthful retail and nightlife hub, famous for the busiest pedestrian crossing on Earth.

## What to See

- **Scramble Crossing** - watch the choreography from the Starbucks overlooking it.
- **Shibuya Sky** - a rooftop observation deck with open-air views across the city.
- **Hachiko Statue** - the classic meeting point outside the station.

## Getting There

Shibuya Station is on the JR Yamanote line and several subway lines. The station has been heavily redeveloped, so follow signage to the Hachiko exit.
