# Yanaka

Yanaka is a quiet old-downtown (shitamachi) area that survived wartime bombing, making it one of the most atmospheric parts of Tokyo.

## What to See

- **Yanaka Ginza** - a low-key shopping street full of snacks and craft shops.
- **Yanaka Cemetery** - leafy lanes lined with cherry trees, peaceful for a stroll.
- **Independent cafes** - the area is a haven for slow coffee and small galleries.

## Getting There

Nippori Station on the JR Yamanote line is the easiest entry point.
