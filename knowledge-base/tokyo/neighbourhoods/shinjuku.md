# Shinjuku

Shinjuku is the beating commercial heart of Tokyo, anchored by the world's busiest railway station.

## What to See

- **Kabukicho** - the neon-lit entertainment district, busiest after dark.
- **Shinjuku Gyoen** - a large, tranquil garden mixing English, French, and Japanese styles. Worth the small entry fee.
- **Omoide Yokocho** - a warren of tiny yakitori bars in the alleys near the west exit.

## Getting There

Shinjuku Station is served by the Marunouchi, Oedo, and Shinjuku subway lines plus the JR Yamanote loop. Use the station's exit numbers to navigate - the building is enormous.


## Omoide Yokocho

Arrive before 18:00 to get a counter seat in the narrow yakitori alleys; many stalls are cash only.
