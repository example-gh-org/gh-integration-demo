# Getting Around Tokyo

Tokyo's public transport is fast, punctual, and dense. This guide covers the essentials for moving around the city.

## IC Cards

Buy a Suica or Pasmo IC card at any station. Tap in and out at turnstiles, and use the same card at convenience stores, vending machines, and lockers. Top up at any ticket machine.

## Metro and Subway

The Tokyo Metro and Toei Subway lines reach almost every neighbourhood. Trains run roughly every 3-5 minutes during the day. Avoid the 8-9am rush if you can.

## Day Trips

The JR Pass covers shinkansen and most JR lines, making day trips to Nikko, Kamakura, and Hakone straightforward. Activate the pass on your first travel day to maximise its value.
