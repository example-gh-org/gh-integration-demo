# Staying Connected in Tokyo

Reliable data makes navigating Tokyo far easier.

## Options

- **eSIM** - the simplest option for most modern phones; activate before arrival.
- **Pocket Wi-Fi** - rent at the airport if several people need to share.
- **Free Wi-Fi** - available in many stations, cafes, and convenience stores, though sign-in can be clunky.

## Useful Apps

A transit routing app and an offline translation app cover most day-to-day needs.
