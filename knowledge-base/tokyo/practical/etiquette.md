# Etiquette in Tokyo

A few simple habits go a long way toward a smooth visit.

## Key Points

- **Queue** - line up neatly for trains, lifts, and shops.
- **Quiet on trains** - keep calls and conversation low.
- **Shoes off** - remove them where you see a step up or a rack at the entrance.
- **No tipping** - it is not expected and can cause confusion.
- **Eating** - generally done sitting down rather than while walking.
