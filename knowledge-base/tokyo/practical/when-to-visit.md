# When to Visit Tokyo

Each season offers something different in Tokyo.

## Seasons

- **Spring** - cherry blossom in late March and early April; beautiful but busy.
- **Summer** - hot and humid, with lively festivals and fireworks.
- **Autumn** - crisp air and colourful foliage; many travellers' favourite.
- **Winter** - cold but dry and clear, with the best Mount Fuji views.

## Dates to Avoid

Golden Week (early May), Obon (mid-August), and New Year see heavy domestic travel.
