# Money and Payments in Tokyo

Tokyo is increasingly cashless, but cash still matters at small shops, shrines, and street stalls.

## Practicalities

- **Cards** - widely accepted in chains and department stores.
- **Cash** - keep some for temples, markets, and older restaurants.
- **ATMs** - convenience store and post office ATMs reliably accept foreign cards.

## Tax-Free Shopping

Many stores offer tax-free purchases for tourists above a minimum spend; bring your passport.
